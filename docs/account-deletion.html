账号删除 / Account deletion

「塑身工坊」（Fat Battle）支持在应用内删除账号，符合 Google Play 账号删除政策。

应用内删除（推荐）

  1. 打开 App → 工坊设置
  2. 进入「账号安全与隐私规范」
  3. 点击 注销账号并抹除个人数据 并确认

注销后账号立即停用，本地数据马上抹除。云端账号进入 30 天软删除，期满后彻底清除。

无法打开 App 时

请发送邮件至 support@bodystudio.app，说明注册邮箱，我们将在核实身份后按同样的 30 天流程处理。

In-app deletion (English)

Open the app → Settings → Account & privacy → Delete account and erase personal data.

The account is disabled immediately. Server-side data is soft-deleted for 30 days, then permanently removed. Local device data is erased at once.

If you cannot open the app, email support@bodystudio.app from the registered address.
